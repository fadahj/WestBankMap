1967

Israel captures the West Bank, east Jerusalem and the Gaza Strip in the Mideast war. Settlement construction begins soon after.

Half a century of settlements

In 56 years, Israel has built well over 100 settlements scattered across the West Bank. Settlers have also built scores of unauthorized outposts, tolerated or even encouraged by the government.

Outposts multiply

From the late 1990s, small unauthorized outposts spread rapidly alongside official settlements — some later legalized retroactively.

Dwindling two-state prospects

With more than 500,000 Israeli settlers now living in the West Bank, partitioning the territory for a two-state solution grows more difficult.
